Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This policy explains what personal information stream (“we”, “us”) collects, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. stream is operated by [YOUR FULL NAME]. You can reach us at privacy@watchstream.app for privacy matters.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us. When you create an account we collect your name, date of birth and email address, and the password you choose. If you sign in with Google, we receive the basic details Google shares with us, such as your name and email address. Your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We never see or store it in readable form.

Information created when you use stream. We save how far you have watched a film (the title and the time you stopped) so you can continue later. We also save your profiles (name, avatar and kids setting) and My List. They are stored on your device and synced to your account in Google Firebase, so they follow you to other devices. Your searches are processed in your browser and are not stored by us.

Technical information. Like any website, the services that run stream receive technical data such as your IP address, browser and device type, and the pages you open. Once we switch on Google Analytics (see section 5), it will also collect usage information such as pages viewed, approximate location (city or country level) and how long you stayed.

What we do not collect. We do not take payments, so we hold no card details. We do not ask for your precise location, contacts or photos.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and run your account and let you sign in.
  • To provide stream, including remembering your watch progress.
  • To check that you meet our minimum age requirement.
  • To keep stream secure and prevent abuse.
  • To understand how stream is used so we can improve it.
  • To answer your questions and requests.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

3. Legal bases (for users in the UK and EEA)

We process your information because it is necessary to provide the service you asked for (contract), because we have a legitimate interest in running, securing and improving stream, because you gave your consent (for example for analytics cookies, where required), or because the law requires it.

4. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers that help us run stream, and only as needed:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore database) stores your account and profile details.
  • Google Analytics (planned) measures usage.
  • GitHub hosts the stream website and receives standard request logs.
  • The Internet Archive delivers the films you watch straight from its servers to your device, so it receives your IP address and device details.
  • Live channel providers deliver live streams directly to your device, so they receive your IP address and device details.
  • Content delivery and image hosts (for example jsDelivr for a video-player library, and the sites that host channel logos) receive your IP address when your browser loads those files.

If we introduce advertising, advertising partners may collect information through ads. We will update this policy and our Cookie Policy before that happens. We may also disclose information if the law requires it or to protect our rights and users.

5. Google Analytics

We plan to use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are popular. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies. Where the law requires, we will ask for your consent first. You can also block it with Google’s opt-out browser add-on or your browser settings. See our Cookie Policy for details.

6. International transfers

stream is run from Nigeria. Our providers, including Google and GitHub, process data in the United States and other countries. Where required, they rely on safeguards such as standard contractual clauses. By using stream you understand that your information may be processed outside your country.

7. How long we keep information

We keep your account information for as long as your account is open. If you ask us to delete your account, we will delete or anonymise your profile within 30 days, unless the law requires us to keep something. Watch progress stored in your browser stays there until you clear your browser data. Analytics data is kept for the shortest period we can configure.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access your information, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its use, receive a copy of it, and withdraw consent at any time. You can also complain to your local data protection authority (for example the Nigeria Data Protection Commission, your national authority in the EEA, or the ICO in the UK).

California residents. You can ask what personal information we hold, ask us to correct or delete it, and you will not be treated differently for doing so. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

To use any of these rights, email privacy@watchstream.app. We may need to confirm your identity first. You can delete your account yourself in the app under Profile, then Account.

9. Children

You must be at least 13 to create an account, and if you are under 18 you need permission from a parent or guardian. In some countries the age of digital consent is higher than 13, up to 16, and in that case you need a parent’s permission. If we learn that we hold information from a child under 13, we will delete it. Parents who think this has happened can contact us at privacy@watchstream.app.

10. Security

We use HTTPS, Google’s authentication system and database rules that let each person read and edit only their own profile. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please use a strong, unique password.

11. Links to other sites

stream links to and plays content from third parties. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, so please read their policies.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the “last updated” date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app or by email.

13. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email privacy@watchstream.app. Please use this address only for privacy matters, such as questions about your information or requests to access, correct or delete it. Emails about other topics may be ignored or rejected. For help using the app, write to support@watchstream.app.
