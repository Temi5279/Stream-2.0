This notice explains where the films and channels on stream come from, who owns them, and how to contact us if you think something should not be here.

1. The films

The films on stream come from the Internet Archive. We choose films that we believe are in the public domain in the United States, for example because they were published before 1931, released without a copyright notice, or their copyright was not renewed. Each film page shows the reason we rely on. We do not host the video files. They stream directly from archive.org to your device.

Public domain depends on the country. A film that is free of copyright in the United States may still be protected in the European Union, the United Kingdom or elsewhere, where copyright often lasts for the life of the creator plus 70 years. Please check your local law before copying or reusing any film. This notice is general information, not legal advice.

If a channel owner does not want its channel on stream, tell us and we will remove it promptly.
