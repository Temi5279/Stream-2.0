Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Stream is free to use. We may add ads in the future to keep it running.

Where do the films come from?

From the Internet Archive's public-domain collection. We choose films that we believe are in the public domain in the United States.

Do I need to install anything?

No, it works in your browser. You can also install it to your home screen if you like.

What do you do with my details?

We use your name, email and date of birth to run your account. We don't sell them.
