Questions

Is stream really free?

Yes. You don't pay to watch. Clearly labelled, non-personalized sponsored ads help cover running costs. Kids profiles don't see ads.

Where do the films come from?

From the Internet Archive's public-domain collection. We choose films that we believe are in the public domain in the United States.

What can I watch on live TV?

Free channels for news, sports, music, kids and more. Some channels only work in their own country, and captions and schedules appear where a channel provides them.

Can I watch without internet?

Yes. Download a film inside the app and it plays offline. Downloads stay inside stream and can't be saved as files.

Which devices work?

Phones, tablets, laptops, desktops and smart TVs with a web browser. You can also install it like an app on your home screen.

Is there a kids mode?

You can create kids profiles. For now they hide horror films, and a full kids experience is coming.

What do you do with my details?

We use your name, email and date of birth to run your account. We don't sell them. Read our Privacy Policy.
