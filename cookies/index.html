Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Cookies are small files stored on your device. Similar technologies, such as browser storage, do the same job. This page explains what stream uses and why.

1. What we use

Name or typePurposeCategoryHow long
Firebase sign-in data (browser storage)Keeps you signed inEssentialUntil you sign out or clear data
stream-progress (local storage)Remembers how far you got in each filmEssentialUntil you clear browser data
Offline cache (service worker)Loads the app faster and keeps pages availableEssentialUntil we publish an update or you clear data
stream-ad-last (local storage)Limits how often a pre-roll ad is shownEssentialUntil cleared; cooldown lasts 10 minutes
_ga, _ga_ (Google Analytics, planned)Counts visitors and pages viewedAnalyticsUp to 2 years

2. Third-party content

When you watch a film or channel, your browser connects to the Internet Archive or the channel’s provider. Your browser also loads some files from other hosts, such as the player library and channel logos. Some live channels play in YouTube's embedded player, which may set cookies when you press play. These companies may set their own cookies or collect your IP address. We do not control them.

3. Analytics

We plan to switch on Google Analytics. Where the law requires, we will ask for your consent before analytics cookies are set. You can also use Google’s opt-out add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

4. Advertising

stream shows a non-personalized sponsored image card on Home and a skippable pre-roll before some films. These ads do not set advertising cookies. A timestamp stored in local storage as stream-ad-last limits pre-rolls to one every 10 minutes and remains until you clear it. If analytics is enabled for your visit, ad impression and click events may be measured as described in our Privacy Policy.

5. Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies and site data in your browser settings. If you clear site data for stream, you will be signed out and your watch progress will be removed. Blocking essential storage may stop stream from working properly.

6. Contact

Questions about cookies? Email privacy@watchstream.app. Please use this address only for privacy and cookie questions, because emails about other topics may be ignored or rejected. For more on how we handle your information, read our Privacy Policy.
