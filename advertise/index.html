What we need from you

Video ad file

An MP4 video, 10 to 30 seconds, ideally 1280×720 (landscape), under 100 MB. Make sure you own the video and the music in it.

Banner ad file

A JPG, PNG or WebP image, 1200×400 pixels (3 to 1 wide), under 300 KB, plus the link people should go to when they tap it.

Our ad rules

No adult content, gambling, weapons, tobacco, misleading claims or anything illegal. We may refuse or remove any ad, and if we can't run yours we refund you.

Questions

Do you charge per view?

No. You pay one flat price for the number of days you choose, with a minimum of 3 days.

Can I choose my own number of days?

Yes. Pick 3, 7, 14 or 30, or type any number from 3 to 90 and the price updates.

No. Ads are never shown on kids profiles.

Do you guarantee views or sales?

No. stream is growing, so we can't promise a number of views, clicks or sales. We promise your ad runs on the days you paid for.

How do I pay?

After you send your enquiry we confirm the details and send a payment link. You can pay in naira or dollars.
